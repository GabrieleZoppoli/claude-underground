## 1. GDPR

**Primary source:** Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — EUR-Lex CELEX 32016R0679
Verify current text at: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

### Checklist — what to verify

- [ ] **Lawful basis** (Art. 6): which basis applies (consent, contract, legal obligation,
      vital interests, public task, legitimate interests)? Is it documented?
- [ ] **Special-category / health data** (Art. 9): is a specific Art. 9(2) ground identified
      (e.g., explicit consent, scientific research — 9(2)(j))? Is it recorded?
- [ ] **Scientific research provisions** (Art. 89): are safeguards proportionate (pseudonymisation,
      data-minimisation)? Is a derogation from data-subject rights invoked and justified?
- [ ] **Controller–processor / DPA** (Art. 28): is a written Data Processing Agreement in place
      for every processor? Does it cover the mandatory elements listed below?
- [ ] **International transfers** (Chapter V): is any transfer to a third country covered by an
      adequacy decision, SCCs, or another Art. 46/47/49 mechanism? Is a TIA completed?
- [ ] **Records of processing** (Art. 30): does the controller maintain a Record of Processing
      Activities (ROPA)? Is this processing activity listed?
- [ ] **DPIA** (Art. 35): does the processing involve large-scale health data or systematic
      monitoring? Has a DPIA been conducted and documented?

### Clause library — Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28 DPA)

| Clause | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Subject-matter & duration | Processing purpose, categories of data, duration — must match the controller's instructions |
| Processing only on instructions | Processor must not process beyond the controller's documented instructions |
| Confidentiality | Processor personnel bound by confidentiality |
| Security measures | Art. 32 technical and organisational measures listed (or referenced by exhibit) |
| Sub-processors | Prior written authorisation by controller; sub-processor bound by same obligations |
| Data-subject rights | Processor assists controller in responding to requests |
| Deletion / return | What happens to data on contract end |
| Audit rights | Controller's right to audit; cooperation with supervisory authority |
| International transfers | Sub-processor transfers addressed; SCCs/TIA if applicable |
| Liability / indemnity | Allocation between controller and processor — confirm with counsel |

## 2. Horizon Europe grant & consortium

**Primary sources:**
- Model Grant Agreement (MGA) — EU Funding & Tenders portal:
  https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/common/agr-contr/general-mga_horizon-euratom_en.pdf
  (verify current version on the portal before relying)
- Consortium Agreement (DESCA model): https://www.desca-agreement.eu/
  (verify current DESCA version — revisions are released periodically)

### Checklist — what to verify

- [ ] **Ownership of results** (MGA Art. 16 / DESCA): which beneficiary owns which results?
      Is ownership clearly allocated per work package or task?
- [ ] **Background** (MGA Art. 3 / DESCA): is background correctly identified and listed?
      Are access-right obligations to background understood?
- [ ] **Access rights** (MGA Arts. 25–26 / DESCA): access rights for implementation vs.
      exploitation — on what terms (royalty-free / fair & reasonable)? Who can refuse?
- [ ] **Dissemination & open science** (MGA Art. 17): open-access publication requirement,
      embargo period, data management plan — are deadlines known?
- [ ] **Ethics requirements** (MGA Art. 34): ethical self-assessment, required deliverables,
      national ethics approvals — timeline against grant milestones?
- [ ] **Liability & governance** (DESCA): internal liability cap between partners; majority
      voting thresholds; coordinator obligations — confirm with consortium counsel.
- [ ] **Exploitation obligations** (MGA Art. 23): plans for use of results; Commission's
      right to object — timelines documented?

### Clause library — Consortium Agreement (DESCA model)

| Clause | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Governance / steering committee | Voting rules, quorum, conflict of interest |
| Internal liability cap | Cap per partner for indirect claims — confirm figure and exclusions |
| IP ownership schedule | Per-task / per-WP ownership — must align with MGA |
| Access-right schedule | Implementation access vs. exploitation access; terms for each |
| Dissemination procedure | Internal review window before submission; veto for IP protection |
| Default / exclusion mechanism | What triggers exclusion; redistribution of tasks |
| Confidentiality | Duration after project end; carve-outs for mandatory disclosure |

## 3. EU Clinical Trials Regulation

**Primary source:** Regulation (EU) 536/2014 — EUR-Lex CELEX 32014R0536
CTIS portal: https://euclinicaltrials.eu/
Verify current requirements, CTIS guidance documents, and national competent authority instructions.

### Checklist — what to verify

- [ ] **Sponsor obligations** (Reg. 536/2014): who is the sponsor; co-sponsorship arrangement?
      Sponsor responsibilities for CTIS submission, safety reporting, protocol amendments.
- [ ] **Clinical Trial Agreement (CTA) elements**: site-specific costs; invoicing milestones;
      IP allocation (who owns data, samples, inventions arising from the trial);
      publication rights and timelines; indemnification by sponsor.
- [ ] **Insurance / compensation** (Reg. 536/2014, Art. 76): adequate compensation mechanism
      in place — national requirements may differ; confirm with site legal team.
- [ ] **Data flows**: does the trial generate personal health data? GDPR Art. 9 + Art. 89
      analysis required — see section 1 above.
- [ ] **Investigational medicinal product (IMP) supply and labelling**: responsibilities
      allocated in CTA? Verify against national competent authority requirements.

> All the above are frameworks for discussion with qualified legal and regulatory counsel.
> Do not rely on this checklist as a substitute for professional review.
