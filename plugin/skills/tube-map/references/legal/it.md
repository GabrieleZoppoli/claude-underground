> Disclaimer: informational paralegal support — not legal advice. Claude is not a lawyer.
> See `README.md` before using this module. Verify every point with qualified counsel.
> Primary sources: Normattiva (https://www.normattiva.it), Gazzetta Ufficiale (https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it).

## 1. Privacy / Garante

**Primary sources:**
- Codice in materia di protezione dei dati personali: D.lgs 30 giugno 2003, n. 196,
  as substantially amended by D.lgs 10 agosto 2018, n. 101 (GDPR-alignment decree).
  Verify current consolidated text on Normattiva.
- Garante per la protezione dei dati personali — guidance documents and provvedimenti
  for scientific research: https://www.garanteprivacy.it/
  (verify current guidance; the Garante issues updated provvedimenti periodically)

### Checklist — what to verify

- [ ] **Applicability of the Codice** (D.lgs 196/2003 as amended): does Italian law add
      requirements beyond GDPR? Check national derogations and the Garante's
      authorizzazioni generali for scientific research (verify current list on Garante website).
- [ ] **Consenso informato per la ricerca**: is explicit consent required, or does a
      Garante research authorisation / waiver apply? Is the consent form compliant with
      both GDPR Art. 7/9 and Garante guidance?
- [ ] **Dati sulla salute** (categorie particolari): are specific Italian safeguards
      (beyond GDPR) triggered? Verify Garante provvedimenti on health data in research.
- [ ] **Trasferimenti verso paesi terzi**: same GDPR Chapter V analysis applies;
      check whether any Italian sectoral rule adds restrictions.
- [ ] **Nomina del Responsabile del Trattamento**: is a written appointment in place
      (Art. 28 GDPR + D.lgs 101/2018 alignment)? Are sub-processors listed?
- [ ] **DPO / RPD**: does the institution have a Data Protection Officer (RPD)?
      Is the processing notified to the RPD?

### Notes on Garante guidance

The Garante has issued specific guidance for scientific research (verify current
provvedimenti at https://www.garanteprivacy.it — search "ricerca scientifica").
Do not assume any particular provvedimento is still in force without live verification.
Confirm with counsel whether a specific research project falls under general authorisation
or requires an ad hoc Garante opinion.

## 2. Public procurement & university contracts

**Primary sources:**
- Codice dei contratti pubblici: D.lgs 31 marzo 2023, n. 36.
  Verify current text on Normattiva.
- MIT (Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti) guidelines and ANAC acts —
  verify current operative guidance.

### Checklist — what to verify

- [ ] **Applicability of D.lgs 36/2023**: does the contract involve a public contracting
      authority? Is the value above the relevant threshold? Which procedure applies?
- [ ] **RUP (Responsabile Unico del Procedimento / del Progetto)**: is a RUP formally
      appointed? Are the RUP's documented responsibilities consistent with the contract
      and D.lgs 36/2023? (Note: D.lgs 36/2023 introduced the "Responsabile Unico del
      Progetto" terminology — verify current operative provisions on Normattiva.)
- [ ] **Convenzione conto terzi / contratto di ricerca**: is the contract governed by
      internal university regulations on conto-terzi activities? Are IP rights, overhead
      rates, and personnel allocation documented per university statute?
- [ ] **Anticorruzione / trasparenza**: ANAC obligations for the contracting authority —
      publication on "Amministrazione Trasparente"?
- [ ] **Subappalto**: are subcontracting limits and conditions from D.lgs 36/2023 respected?
      Verify current provisions — limits have been subject to legislative amendments.

### Clause library — contratto di ricerca / convenzione conto terzi

| Clause | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Oggetto e durata | Clear scope; milestones; renewal conditions |
| Corrispettivo e overhead | Overhead rate aligned with university regulations; VAT treatment |
| Proprietà dei risultati | Who owns IP arising from the research — university policy + contract must align |
| Pubblicazione | Right to publish; embargo; sponsor review window |
| Riservatezza | Duration; carve-out for regulatory / institutional obligations |
| Responsabilità | Limitation of liability; indemnification — confirm with institutional legal office |
| Recesso | Termination for cause vs. convenience; notice period |
| Foro competente | Jurisdiction — for public contracts, administrative court may be relevant |

## 3. Clinical research

**Primary sources:**
- Consenso informato: D.lgs 24 giugno 2003, n. 211 (attuazione Direttiva 2001/20/CE)
  and Regulation (EU) 536/2014 (see eu.md). Verify current national transposition.
- Comitati etici territoriali: D.lgs 14 maggio 2019, n. 52 and subsequent AIFA acts —
  verify current list and competence of territorial ethics committees on AIFA website.
- AIFA (Agenzia Italiana del Farmaco): https://www.aifa.gov.it/
  For IMP trials, verify current AIFA submission requirements.

### Checklist — what to verify

- [ ] **Consenso informato**: is the consent form compliant with current national requirements
      and Reg. 536/2014? Is it approved by the competent comitato etico? Language (Italian)?
- [ ] **Comitato etico territoriale competente**: which CET has territorial competence for
      the site? Is the submission complete per CTIS + national requirements?
      Verify current CET list and competence map on AIFA.
- [ ] **AIFA notification / authorisation** (for IMP clinical trials): has AIFA been notified
      or authorised as required? Verify current procedure on AIFA website — requirements
      depend on trial phase and IMP status.
- [ ] **Assicurazione**: is an adequate insurance / compensation mechanism in place per
      D.lgs 211/2003 and Reg. 536/2014 Art. 76? Verify Italian national requirements.
- [ ] **Trattamento dei dati** (salute dei partecipanti): GDPR Art. 9 + Garante guidance
      for clinical research — see section 1 above.

> All the above are frameworks for discussion with qualified legal and regulatory counsel.
> Do not rely on this checklist as a substitute for professional review of specific documents.
